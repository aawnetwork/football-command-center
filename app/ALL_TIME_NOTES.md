# All-Time data sources

## CFB
- NCAA FBS Football Record Book: https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/football_records/FBS.pdf
- College Football at Sports-Reference career leaderboards: https://www.football-reference.com/cfb/leaders/
- The displayed CFB leaders are historical career leaderboards, not current-season totals.

## NFL
- NFL 2024 Record & Fact Book: https://operations.nfl.com/media/hfjn4oid/2024-record-fact-book-incl-supplemental.pdf
- The NFL book says the records are compiled by the Elias Sports Bureau and reflect available official NFL information from the league's formation in 1920, including applicable AFL records from 1960-69.
- The displayed NFL leaders are a record-book snapshot, not current-season totals.

## Local completion review — 22 October 2026

### Supported tables and scope

- NFL Individual: nine Career and Season categories, six populated Single Game categories (passing yards, rushing yards, receiving yards, yards from scrimmage, tackles, sacks).
- CFB Individual: eleven Career and Season categories, four populated Single Game categories (passing, rushing, receiving and total offense yards). Original individual observations are unchanged. CFB Season passing touchdowns has 24 retained rows and interceptions has 20; other Individual tables have 25 rows.
- NFL Team All-Time: Wins and Winning Percentage, plus Games Played, calculated solely as wins + losses + ties in the same 32 retained franchise rows. These are regular-season franchise histories, not modern team-stat sums.
- CFB Team All-Time: Wins and Winning Percentage, with 25 retained programs per table. These are not all current FBS schools, and program history can predate FBS membership.
- Team Single Season is unavailable in both leagues. It is visibly disabled rather than selecting an empty table. Required evidence is an official historical team-season leaderboard with its season, scope, units, eligibility and publication cutoff. A 2026 season-to-date table is not a substitute.
- Equal values use competition ranks (1, 1, 3). Display includes ties at the tenth-row boundary. Invalid/missing values are excluded, not converted to zero. A historical season/game year does not establish active-player status; Career retains its existing source-supplied active flag or 2026 career-span indication, clearly distinguished from live play.

### Cutoff and provenance limitations

The legacy Team files label their rows “Through 2025”, but the retained CFB monitor copy labels identical rows “Through 2024”. The documented NFL source is a **2024** book and does not substantiate the displayed 2025 cutoff. Therefore the UI marks Team cutoffs unverified, without altering source observations or inventing a replacement cutoff. Individual book publication cutoffs were not retained either; record years identify a career/season/game, not the publication date.

The owner approved exactly two official-PDF requests in this task. NCAA `FBS.pdf` failed during connection (no response body); the documented NFL URL returned HTTP 404. Neither PDF was obtained. No retries, replacement-source discovery or other provider collection occurred. More historical Team categories and Team Single Season remain source-blocked. Obtain the official books from the owner, or separately authorise a bounded replacement-URL lookup/retrieval; do not populate those tables from memory.

### How updates actually propagate

1. **Bundled tables:** the read-only public feed now reconciles against the deployed catalog on every read. A stale monitor copy cannot override a bundled correction, omit a newly bundled category or truncate the NFL franchise population to 25. Review an official source, edit the existing data module, preserve its scope/cutoff, run focused tests/build, then obtain normal deployment approval. No database reset or refresh is needed for those corrections. No automatic historical source collection is implied.
2. **NFL active Career:** the existing Tuesday Vercel cron configuration uses nflverse roster selection and ESPN career totals for candidates already in the record book. A successful existing refresh writes the existing monitor publication. The public feed reconciles only verified active candidates into the latest bundled career tables, preserving deployed retired-player corrections and filling the full leaderboard. It accepts those entries only under the verified career catalog version and only for NFL Individual Career tables, with a valid check time and finite observations. Corrections may decrease a total. Per-table source metadata distinguishes this from the bundled snapshots. The browser re-reads the published feed every five minutes; it does not collect athlete histories.
3. **CFB:** Monday's existing cron republishes bundled records; it does **not** discover new college records. No automatic CFB historical updater has been added. Team and Individual Season/Single Game tables in both leagues remain reviewed snapshots.
4. **Failure:** failed provider refreshes retain the previous publication. Unreadable storage or invalid publications fall back to bundled rows without inventing a successful check date. No provider is fetched by the public leaderboard read.

Cron configuration was inspected, not operational execution history. No cron/refresh endpoint, real database request or database write was performed during this task. Tests use an in-memory fake storage response and retained real observations. The existing storage schema, authentication and cron schedules are unchanged.

## Automatic NFL Team updating — 23 October 2026

This section supersedes the earlier **NFL Team cutoff unverified** limitation. CFB's cutoff/source limitations remain.

- **Verified baseline:** all 32 Pro Football Hall of Fame Team Facts pages explicitly say “At Start of 2026 Season”: regular-season franchise W–L–T through 2025. The owner-approved collection used the team index and 32 sequential pages, no retries. Evidence, exact source URLs and HTML hashes are retained in `app/lib/all-time-nfl-baseline.json`; raw captures are private in `/private/tmp/aaw-hof-baseline-2025/`. The download-budget upper bound was 3,162,830 bytes (under 5 MB).
- **Discrepancy:** 31 retained W–L–T rows agreed. San Francisco's published baseline is **642–531–16**, versus the old unverified **644–531–16**. The new baseline follows the explicitly dated source. Winning percentages are recalculated as `(wins + ties/2) / (wins + losses + ties)`; they are not copied from the old rounded percentage table.
- **Runtime:** the existing NFL Tuesday 12:00 UTC cron now refreshes Team and Individual publications independently. The team loader reads the existing nflverse schedule CSV, compares new/corrected results with exact-game final observations from ESPN weekly scoreboards, and stores the confirmed ESPN game IDs/results in the existing `all_time_monitor_state.state` JSON. No migration, new table, schedule or paid service is required. No provider collection runs on public page reads.
- **Bounds:** one schedule request plus at most 18 weekly confirmation requests, 5 MB combined, 15-second per-request timeouts within a 45-second team-source deadline, redirects rejected and no retries. The canonical raw.githubusercontent.com address for the same existing schedule file avoids GitHub redirects; its HEAD connectivity check returned HTTP 200, without collecting a body. Unchanged final games need no new ESPN confirmation. First successful publication catches up to the published confirmed results; it is not a live leaderboard. The configuration alone does not prove a deployed cron has run.
- **Correctness:** totals are always recomputed from the pinned 2025 baseline and the retained game-ID ledger, never incremented on each check. Finals missing from a later schedule/scoreboard retain their last verified values. Changed results require another matching final ESPN observation. Missing/invalid scores are not zero. Conflicting identities, periods, duplicate observations or an unverified baseline change prevent publication. All-time franchise histories remain distinct (including Cleveland/Baltimore); only explicit existing abbreviation aliases are accepted. Postseason games are excluded. The baseline is not replaced at a season rollover; persisted game IDs prevent recounting older seasons.
- **Persistence:** the existing `updated_at` column is used as an optimistic concurrency filter. A concurrent refresh cannot overwrite newer state; zero affected rows fail without retry. Initial insertion ignores a conflicting existing row rather than overwriting it. Both-provider failure writes nothing; one successful pipeline retains the other's last publication. Source failures preserve the last verified Team ledger and expose its last-successful-update time.
- **Individual:** the existing NFL career source still uses nflverse roster selection and ESPN career totals for existing candidates. It is independent of Team failures, and Team updating is independent of career failures. New candidates are not automatically discovered. Retired-player, Season and Single Game books remain snapshots.
- **CFB blocker:** the retained NCAA baseline still has contradictory cutoffs and unverified adjustments. The official book could not be retrieved in the bounded source checks. NCAA's published program definition includes bowl/playoff games and vacated/adjusted records, unlike the NFL regular-season definition. ESPN regular-season outcomes alone cannot establish or maintain those adjusted all-time records. No CFB accumulator is enabled. Required next evidence is the official adjusted program W–L–T baseline with its exact cutoff, stable team crosswalk and a trustworthy publication/correction source; further collection requires separate scope/approval.

Validation uses the real retained 2026 Dallas 37–20 Washington result, its exact ESPN final observation, all 32 newly verified baseline pages, and controlled ties/corrections/rollover/failure/concurrency cases. Tests use a fake JSON persistence endpoint; no production database access or cron invocation occurs. Deployment and an observed first scheduled production publication remain owner-approved activation steps, not completed operations in this local task.

Storage outage safeguard: a configured but unreadable NFL publication returns an error rather than silently resetting the displayed Team totals to 2025. The browser keeps its last received records, labels the publication unavailable, and retains the last-successful-update time. A first visit without a readable publication can display only the explicitly labelled verified baseline.

NFL percentage definition: the NFL-hosted [All-Time Win/Loss Records note](https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/v1599262341/league/yythbdzxfwzbjfmdkxrd.pdf) explicitly applies half-wins/half-losses to ties across all seasons in its all-time percentages. This is distinct from how pre-1972 season standings were originally calculated; those historical Season books are unchanged. The new Team table sorts the calculated ratio, not the legacy rounded percentage entries.
